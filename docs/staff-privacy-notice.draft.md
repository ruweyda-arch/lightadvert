# Staff Privacy Notice — DRAFT

> **DRAFT for Light Advert to review, complete, and adopt.** Not legal advice.

> personal data and individual performance data (Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019).

## Who this is for

Everyone with a Light Advert staff or admin account in the internal work-tracking
system at `[app URL]`.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name and email address.
- **Work records:** the tasks assigned to you, their project, effort estimate,
  status changes, deadlines, the links (deliverables) and comments you add.
- **Contribution records:** for each task an Admin approves, the effort points and
  the approval date, aggregated into per-period and lifetime totals.
- **Change history (audit log):** who changed a pay-affecting value (effort
  estimate, assignee, stamped role, approvals, period locks), when, the old and new
  values, and the reason given.
- **Security data:** a hashed password (never stored in readable form), session
  records, and login-attempt counters used to rate-limit sign-in.

## Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

- To **assign and track creative work** — necessary for performing your contract of
  engagement with Light Advert.
- To **measure contribution and inform pay decisions** — the contribution totals are
  used to allocate the contribution-weighted bonus pool. Legal basis: performance of
  your contract and Light Advert's legitimate interest in paying fairly and
  transparently.
- To **keep an auditable record** of pay-affecting changes — legitimate interest in
  accuracy and dispute resolution.
- To **secure the system** — legitimate interest in protecting staff data.

We do **not** use this data for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly
significant effects without human involvement; an Admin decides pay.

## Who can see it

- **You** can see your own tasks, deliverables, comments, and your own contribution
  totals and history. You cannot see other staff members' numbers.
- **Admins** can see all of the above for everyone, plus the audit log.
- The data is hosted with `[hosting provider, e.g. Supabase / Vercel]` on our behalf
  and is not sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes. Email is sent
  via `[Resend]`; error diagnostics via `[Sentry]`.

## How long we keep it

- **Contribution history and the audit log are kept indefinitely**, because they
  support pay decisions and any later dispute.
- If you leave, your account is **deactivated** (you can no longer sign in) but your
  historical records are retained for the reasons above.
- `[State any fixed retention period Light Advert decides on, if different.]`

## Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to access the personal data we hold
about you, to correct it if it is wrong, and — subject to our need to keep pay and
audit records — to have it erased or its use restricted. You can also object to
processing based on legitimate interest and lodge a complaint with the Office of the
Data Protection Commissioner.
